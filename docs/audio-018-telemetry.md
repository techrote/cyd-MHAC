Run `python tools/audio/telemetry_review.py session.txt`. Exit 0 means CLEAR_OBSERVED_INDICATORS (only observed counters/maxima); 1 means HOLD_INDICATORS (nonzero error/watchdog/failed/over counter or preparation >= 5334 us); 2 means INSUFFICIENT (missing/malformed data, inadequate block span, detected reboot, decrease/wrap, or invalid values). Invalid data takes precedence but flags are retained. A log without startup marker may be reviewed but cannot establish a fresh boot. Nominal seconds are completed-block span times 128/24000, not physical duration.
